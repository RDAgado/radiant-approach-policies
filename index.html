Pet Taxi Meter Privacy Policy

Publisher: Radiant Approach LTD

Effective date: 2026-09-30

Publisher contact: apps@radiantapproach.com

What Pet Taxi Meter does

Pet Taxi Meter is an Android-first device-local fare meter for pet transport operators. It lets an operator configure rates, measure an active ride, review a fare, save completed trips, and prepare a receipt for handoff to another app.

Information stored on the device

The app stores settings and trip information on the device, including the selected meter look, rates and optional charges, operator or business name, active-trip state, elapsed time, filtered distance, fare breakdowns, and completed trips. The app does not provide an account system, developer backend, analytics service, advertising SDK, map service, or cloud trip synchronisation.

Location

When the operator starts a ride and grants Android location permission, the app uses location updates through an Android location foreground service to estimate GPS distance for that active ride. Location is processed by the app for the ride calculation. This implementation has no developer-server upload path. Location collection is intended to stop when the ride is stopped; Android displays an ongoing notification while the service is active.

Backup and restore

The operator can export rates, the selected meter look, operator or business name, and completed trips (including their stored fare breakdown and rate snapshot) to a JSON file chosen through Android's document picker. The operator controls the destination and any later sharing of that file. The app can read a user-selected backup, validate it, and replace the corresponding local snapshot. The app does not upload backups to a server.

Email and WhatsApp handoff

From a completed trip, the operator can edit an itemised receipt and ask Android to open a chosen Email or WhatsApp handoff. The receipt text is transferred to the external app selected by the operator. Pet Taxi Meter does not send the message itself and does not mark it as sent merely because the external app opens. The external app's own privacy policy and handling rules apply after handoff.

Sharing, retention, and deletion

Pet Taxi Meter does not share locally stored values with Radiant Approach LTD through a server in this implementation. Data can leave the device if the operator exports a backup or explicitly hands receipt text to another app. Local data remains subject to the device and app storage controls. The publisher has not made a separate retention or deletion promise beyond those local controls.

Permissions

The app requests location permission for active ride measurement and notification permission where Android requires it for the ongoing ride notification. The app's behavior may be limited if these permissions are denied or if device location is disabled.

Changes and contact

This policy should be reviewed and updated if the app's data flows change. Questions or privacy requests should be sent to the publisher contact above.
